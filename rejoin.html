Rejoin a split document

When a client's document can't reach the firm's document storage, the intake form emails it instead. Anything larger than 3 MB is split across several emails, with attachments named like statement.pdf.part01-of-04.

  1. Download every attachment from those emails (all the parts of one file).
  2. Select or drag them all into the box below.
  3. Click Download original to get the file back, ready to open.
Drop the parts here, or click to choose
Select all parts at once · you can add more afterwards

Everything happens inside this browser tab. The parts are not uploaded, sent, or saved anywhere.
